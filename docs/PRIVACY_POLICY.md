# SL Launcher Pro Privacy Policy (draft)

_Draft for the publisher to review and adapt before publication. Last updated: 20 November 2026._

SL Launcher Pro ("SL") is a home-screen app for Android. This policy explains what SL does with information on your phone.

## Summary

- SL has **no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics**.
- Your layout, notes, tasks, settings and history stay **on your phone**.
- SL uses the internet only to download **news headlines and images** from the publishers you see in the News screen, and when you open a link.
- Features that read personal data (notifications, contacts, calendar, music, microphone) work only after you allow them, and you can turn each one off at any time.

## Information SL processes on your phone

| Information | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Installed apps | Showing and launching your apps | Stays on the phone |
| Notifications (if you grant notification access) | Dynamic Island, notification center, quick replies, badges | Stays on the phone. Optional history is kept for 7 days and can be cleared |
| Microphone audio (while you talk to SL) | Turning speech into text | Your phone's speech service. On phones with on-device recognition, audio stays on the phone. Otherwise the phone's speech provider (for example Google) processes it under its own privacy policy |
| Contacts (if allowed) | Contact search and quick contacts | Stays on the phone |
| Calendar (if allowed) | Today's events | Stays on the phone |
| Music files (if allowed) | Playing your songs | Stays on the phone |
| Notes, tasks, habits, countdowns, search history | Productivity features | Stays on the phone |
| App launch times (last 30 days) | Suggesting apps for the time of day | Stays on the phone. Reset in Privacy |
| Screen time (if you grant usage access) | Showing today's per-app usage | Read when you open Screen time; not stored |
| Gesture helper (if you turn on the accessibility service) | Locking the screen and opening the notification shade | Performs those two actions only. It reads no screen content and no events |

## Internet use

When you open News or it refreshes in the background, SL requests public news feeds and article images from the publishers listed in the app and from any feed addresses you add yourself. Those publishers receive standard web request information (your IP address and a generic app identifier) under their own privacy policies. SL sends no personal information to them.

## Backups

**Android backup:** if your phone's Android backup is on, Android backs up SL's settings and database (home layout, notes, tasks, habits, playlists) to your own Google account and restores them on a new phone. That backup is managed by Android and Google, not by SL, and SL cannot read it.

**Export:** when you export a backup, SL writes a file to the place you choose. That file is yours to keep safe.

## Children

SL is a general-audience app and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Your choices

- **Revoke permissions:** Android Settings › Apps › SL Launcher Pro › Permissions.
- **Turn off notification access:** Android Settings › Notifications › Notification access (or Device & app notifications).
- **Delete all SL data:** Android Settings › Apps › SL Launcher Pro › Storage › Clear storage.
